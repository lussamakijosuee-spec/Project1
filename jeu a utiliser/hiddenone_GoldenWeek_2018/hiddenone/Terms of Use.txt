Kid generator parts for a kimono and yukata. 

Just drag & drop the folders into your generator to add them, and restart your MV to see them in the generator.

If they aren't appearing in your generator, please make sure your MV version is at least 1.3.2.  Renumbering them so they are in numerical order for your generator may be necessary.
